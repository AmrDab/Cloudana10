1. What this is

2. Testnet means testnet

  • Testnet CLD and testnet credits have no monetary value. They exist to test the protocol, may be wiped at any time, cannot be bought, sold, redeemed or transferred for value, and no representation is made that they will ever convert to anything of value. There is no points, airdrop or reward formula attached to testnet activity; at most, reliable providers may be recognised as "founding providers" on terms published before any mainnet.
  • Nothing on this site is investment advice, a securities offering, or a promise about any future token or its price.
  • Payments. Card payments are off during testnet. Cloudana never holds customer assets other than CLD placed in escrow for a job; any swap from another asset happens inside your own wallet.

3. Your responsibilities

  • Run only lawful workloads. No malware, no attacks on third parties, no content that's illegal where you or the provider are located.
  • Providers and verifiers act honestly. Misreporting hardware, faking jobs or verifier results, or attempting to forge certificates is a violation of these terms and will get a node unbound and banned.
  • You are responsible for what you host. Sites and containers you deploy are served by independent node operators. Don't deploy anything illegal, infringing, or harmful; we and operators may stop a deployment that is (section 4). Hosting is probed for reachability and a content hash, not reviewed for content, and the testnet gives no uptime guarantee.
  • Keep your keys safe. Wallets and private keys are yours; we can't recover them and aren't responsible for what happens if you lose them.

4. Abuse, takedowns and DMCA

Sites and containers you deploy are served by independent node operators through a Cloudana gateway. To report illegal, infringing or harmful content, email abuse@cloudana.io with the deployment address and what you are reporting; for copyright, include the information a DMCA notice requires (the work, its location, your contact details and a good-faith statement). The process is: report → review → stop. We review each report, and when it is founded we stop the deployment from the orchestrator so nodes cease serving it; the deployer is told why. Unfounded reports are closed with a reply. Deployers whose content is stopped repeatedly are repeat infringers and lose the right to deploy. Operators may also stop a deployment on their own hardware at any time.

5. On-chain actions are permanent

Transactions on Base are public and irreversible. We cannot undo, edit, or delete anything you commit on-chain.
