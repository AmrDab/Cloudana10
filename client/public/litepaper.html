Litepaper

Useful compute, proven.

Cloudana is a decentralized datacenter. For compute, the work that earns a provider's CLD is the user's computation.

Independent providers contribute CPUs, GPUs, and servers. One orchestrator assigns all the work and sets the price from supply and demand — users never pick a node, and providers don't compete on price. Three roles keep it honest: users pay for work, providers do it, and verifiers check it. For matrix workloads, Cloudana's Proof of Useful Work makes the proof and the useful result the same operation.

01 · Method

How it works

01

Flash and go. A provider installs the node agent once. It detects CPU, RAM, disk, and GPU, runs a benchmark, and binds to the provider's wallet with a one-time code. No spec forms, no price lists.

02

You say what to run; the orchestrator places it. Submit a job or a site. The orchestrator picks a node that fits by a random draw, sets the price, and handles placement. You never choose a provider.

03

Proof = product. For matrix workloads, the provider proves the work by computing it. The proof's hardness comes from the full transcript of the computation, and the decoded result is the answer you paid for. Checked by a σ-bound transcript, Freivalds, and planted tasks.

02 · Thesis

Why the transcript is the proof

The hardness of Cloudana's proof comes from the full transcript of intermediate computation blocks — not from the answer. You can shortcut the answer with low-rank tricks; you cannot shortcut the transcript. So the proof is only valid if the provider genuinely did the work.

And that work is the user's matrix multiply — so the same operation yields a proof you can't fake and a result somebody actually wanted. Because the orchestrator assigns paid inputs, the provider can't swap in matrices of its own choosing.

Why it mattersProof-of-work chains spend electricity on puzzles nobody asked for. Cloudana's difficulty lives inside paid work — energy in, useful computation out, proof attached.

After Komargodski & Weinstein, Proofs of Useful Work from Arbitrary Matrix Multiplication (2025). Our construction is cuPOW; the module is public in the repository.

04 · Services

What runs today

  • Live on testnet: GPU / CPU compute (verified matrix jobs) and static hosting — node agents serve your site through a Cloudana gateway and the orchestrator probes its content hash every minute. Hosting is paid by fees only, with no subsidy.
  • Early access: containers (hardened fleet nodes only), AI inference, storage.
  • Planned: CDN, DNS, TLS, DDoS shielding, WAF, edge functions, object storage, KV & queues, tunnels, zero-trust access, databases, confidential VMs, a website builder, an AI gateway, and desktops for agents.
05 · The token

How CLD is issued

CLD is the unit of account for compute on Cloudana: every job is priced and settled in CLD. When a verified job is paid for, the fee is burned and the provider who did the work is minted most of it — so new CLD exists only because someone paid for real, verified computation:

  • Burn the fee, mint for the verified job. The whole fee is burned at settlement; the provider is minted 95 % of it and the treasury 3 %. A small, capped, declining subsidy is added only when the orchestrator's random draw picked a provider in a genuinely independent cluster — so routing a job to yourself doesn't pay.
  • Priced from utilization. Compute is priced in nano-CLD per tera-MAC plus a small per-job base fee; the price moves with utilization, with no oracle. USD is shown for display only.
  • Minted per verified job, settled on Base in batches. Each epoch (hourly on testnet, daily at mainnet) is posted as a Merkle root, with a veto window and a guardian before claims open.
  • Paying. If you hold another asset, your own wallet can swap it into CLD at the moment you pay; Cloudana never holds it. Cards are off during testnet. One static site per verified identity is free, paid for by the ecosystem treasury at normal fees.
  • No stake to lose. A misbehaving node is unbound and banned. Verifier credits are points, not CLD.

Mainnet genesis is 100M CLD with on-chain vesting (whitepaper §6); testnet uses a fresh 1M token. Testnet CLD has no monetary value and there is no points or airdrop formula (see Terms §2). Supply depends on usage; see the 20-year simulation (a simulation, not a forecast). Nothing about CLD is a promise of price or return.

06 · Status

Status, stated plainly

Cloudana today is an orchestrator-coordinated testnet. Verification is the orchestrator's check of each transcript plus re-checks by browser verifiers, who are also handed planted tasks with known answers. An on-chain Groth16 verifier is in development; until it ships, the orchestrator stays in the critical path, and we say so.

We'll claim decentralized verification when it's true, not before. Before CLD carries value, the contracts, the issuance rules, and these pages will be reviewed and versioned in the public repository.

Errata policyCorrections land in the public repository's history — nothing is edited silently.
07 · Differentiators

What's different

  • Proof of Useful Work for compute — proof and product coincide.
  • One orchestrator, one price — the price comes from utilization, and placement is a random draw.
  • Fee-first issuance — every fee burned, CLD minted per verified job.
  • Plug-and-play onboarding — hardware detection, not configuration.
